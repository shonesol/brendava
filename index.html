How we work

We are a licensed Kampala-based team. We design tailor-made gorilla treks and wildlife safaris across Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania — with a strong focus on authentic experiences, local partnerships and responsible tourism.

Real local relationships

We work with the same trusted guides, drivers and community partners year after year — including Batwa cultural experiences around Bwindi. You meet the people who actually live in these places.

Conservation & community first

Supporting wildlife conservation and the communities that live alongside the parks is part of how we operate — not just a line on a website. Your journey helps sustain the places and people you visit.

Tailor-made, not template

Every itinerary is built around your interests, pace and group. Whether it's a focused gorilla trek or a multi-country circuit, we plan it with local knowledge and clear communication from start to finish.

Top Destinations

Swipe or use the arrows to explore our most popular parks and experiences.

Bwindi Impenetrable NP

World-famous mountain gorilla trekking in Uganda's misty rainforest. Home to roughly half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas. Treks last 2–8 hours. Combine with Batwa cultural experiences and birding.

Enquire about Bwindi

Murchison Falls NP

Uganda's largest national park. Powerful Nile waterfall, Big Five game drives, boat cruise to the base of the falls, and excellent birding including the rare shoebill.

Enquire about Murchison

Queen Elizabeth NP

Famous for tree-climbing lions in the Ishasha sector, the Kazinga Channel boat cruise, hippos, elephants and crater lakes. Easy to combine with Bwindi or Kibale.

Enquire about Queen Elizabeth

Kidepo Valley NP

One of Africa's most pristine and remote parks. Dramatic landscapes, cheetahs, ostriches, lions and very few other vehicles — perfect for exclusive game viewing.

Enquire about Kidepo

Serengeti NP

Witness the Great Migration of over a million wildebeest and zebra across the endless plains of Tanzania. Combine with Ngorongoro or a multi-country itinerary.

Enquire about Serengeti

Ngorongoro Crater

The world's largest intact volcanic caldera — a natural amphitheatre packed with lions, rhinos, elephants and flamingos. A must on any northern Tanzania circuit.

Enquire about Ngorongoro

Masai Mara

Kenya's premier reserve for big cats and the famous Mara River crossings. Peak migration season July–October. Excellent for photography and family safaris.

Enquire about Masai Mara

Volcanoes NP (Rwanda)

High-altitude gorilla encounters with excellent infrastructure. Combine with Uganda's Bwindi for a dual-country gorilla experience. Golden monkey walks also available.

Enquire about Volcanoes
