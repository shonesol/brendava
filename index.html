Top Destinations

Gorilla trekking in Bwindi & Volcanoes, Big Five safaris, the Great Migration and authentic cultural encounters — all under one experienced operator.

Bwindi Impenetrable NP

World-famous mountain gorilla trekking in Uganda’s misty rainforest.

Bwindi is home to roughly half of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas. Treks last 2–8 hours depending on the group. Combine with Batwa cultural experiences and birding. Perfect for 1–3 day extensions or full Uganda itineraries.

Enquire

Murchison Falls NP

Big Five, powerful Nile waterfall and classic game drives.

Uganda’s largest national park. Boat cruise to the base of the falls, game drives for lions, elephants, giraffes and the rare shoebill. Ideal 2–4 day safari stop.

Enquire

Queen Elizabeth NP

Tree-climbing lions, Kazinga Channel boat cruise & crater lakes.

Famous for tree-climbing lions in Ishasha sector, hippos, elephants and excellent birding. Combine easily with Bwindi or Kibale.

Enquire

Kidepo Valley NP

Remote wilderness, cheetahs, ostriches and dramatic landscapes.

One of Africa’s most pristine parks. Excellent for photography and exclusive game viewing with very few other vehicles.

Enquire

Serengeti NP

The Great Migration and endless plains of Tanzania.

Witness over a million wildebeest, zebra and predators. Combine with Ngorongoro or a multi-country itinerary including Uganda or Kenya.

Enquire

Ngorongoro Crater

World’s largest intact volcanic caldera packed with wildlife.

Dense concentrations of lions, rhinos, elephants and flamingos inside a natural amphitheatre. A must on any northern Tanzania circuit.

Enquire

Masai Mara

Big cats and the famous Mara River crossings.

Kenya’s premier reserve for lion, leopard and cheetah sightings. Peak migration season July–October. Excellent for photography and family safaris.

Enquire

Volcanoes NP (Rwanda)

Gorilla trekking and golden monkey walks in Rwanda.

High-altitude gorilla encounters with excellent infrastructure. Combine with Uganda’s Bwindi for a dual-country gorilla experience.
