MEET OUR PROFESSIONALS

Stories From Our Team

Our guides and team members share their passion for Uganda’s wildlife and culture.

Brenda

Managing Director & Safari Specialist

Meet Brenda

Born and raised in Entebbe, Brenda holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Tourism and Hospitality Management and has over five years of professional experience. She has travelled to Kenya, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Qatar, Dubai, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, giving her a strong understanding of international travellers’ expectations.

Brenda oversees gorilla permits, itinerary design and community partnerships. She is passionate about wildlife conservation and creating genuine connections between guests and the places they visit.

“Seeing the excitement on a traveler’s face when they spot a lion for the first time or come face-to-face with mountain gorillas reminds me why I love this work. Every adventure is unique.”

Andy

Assistant Manager & Safari Guide

Meet Andy

Born and raised in Entebbe, Andy has spent the past five years creating unforgettable safari experiences. His friendly personality and deep knowledge of Uganda’s wildlife make every journey both educational and exciting. He has also travelled to Kenya, Tanzania, Dubai, Ethiopia, Qatar, the UK and Switzerland.

Whether guiding guests through Bwindi or leading game drives, Andy is dedicated to safe, memorable and inspiring safaris.

“The greatest reward is seeing a guest smile after experiencing Uganda’s incredible wildlife. Every safari is a new story.”

Shone

Reservations • Tour Operations • Technology

Meet Shone

Shone combines tourism expertise with technology. He handles reservations, logistics and digital systems while also guiding and coordinating safaris. He has travelled widely across East Africa, Europe and the Middle East, bringing a practical and guest-focused approach to every booking.

“Travel has the power to connect people, cultures and nature. My mission is to create unforgettable journeys that inspire every traveler.”
