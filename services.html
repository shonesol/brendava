Our Services

Brendava Tours & Travel

We design private and small-group journeys focused on mountain gorilla trekking, classic East African wildlife safaris and authentic cultural experiences. Every itinerary is built around your interests, pace and group size, with clear communication and reliable local partners from start to finish.

What We Offer

Gorilla Trekking

Guided mountain gorilla treks in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park (Uganda) and Volcanoes National Park (Rwanda). We handle permits, transport, accommodation and experienced trackers so you can focus on the encounter.

Wildlife Safaris

Game drives and boat cruises in Murchison Falls, Queen Elizabeth, Kidepo Valley and other top parks. Spot the Big Five, tree-climbing lions, elephants, hippos and rare species with knowledgeable local guides.

Multi-Country East Africa

Seamless itineraries combining Uganda with Rwanda, Kenya (Masai Mara) and Tanzania (Serengeti & Ngorongoro). Ideal for travellers who want the best of the region in one well-planned journey.

Chimpanzee Tracking

Walk with chimpanzees in Kibale Forest and other primate habitats. A perfect addition to gorilla trekking or a dedicated primate-focused trip with expert trackers.

Cultural Experiences

Authentic community visits, including Batwa cultural walks around Bwindi, local markets, traditional food and meaningful interactions with the people who live alongside the parks.

Tailor-Made Itineraries

Every trip is designed around you — whether you want a short gorilla-focused stay, a family safari, a honeymoon or a longer multi-country circuit. Private or small-group options available.

Birding Tours

Uganda is one of Africa’s top birding destinations. Join guided birding trips targeting rare and endemic species with specialist local bird guides.

Honeymoon & Special Trips

Romantic, carefully paced itineraries for couples — combining gorilla encounters, scenic lodges, private dinners and the most memorable moments of East Africa.

Airport Transfers & Logistics

Reliable airport pick-ups and drop-offs, inter-park transfers and full ground logistics so your journey runs smoothly from arrival to departure.

Lodge & Hotel Arrangements

We book carefully selected lodges and hotels that match your style and budget — from comfortable mid-range to higher-end properties near the parks.

Adventure Activities

Optional additions such as white-water rafting on the Nile, nature walks, crater lake visits and short hikes that complement your main safari or trek.

Full Trip Support

From the first enquiry through permits, payments and on-ground assistance, our Kampala-based team stays available so you always have a clear point of contact.

At Brendava Tours & Travel we focus on genuine experiences, transparent pricing and personal service. Tell us what you would like to see and do — we will shape a clear proposal around it.
