Payment Policy

1. Booking Deposit

To secure your reservation with Brendava Tours, a non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total tour price is required at the time of booking.

Note: Your booking is only confirmed once the deposit has been received and a confirmation email has been sent to you.

2. Balance Payment

The remaining balance must be paid no later than 30 days before the tour start date.

  • For bookings made within 30 days of the departure date, full payment is required at the time of booking.
  • We will send a friendly reminder email 40 days before departure.

3. Accepted Payment Methods

  • Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Bank Transfer (details provided upon request)
  • PayPal
  • Mobile Money (selected destinations)

4. Currency & Pricing

All prices are quoted in USD unless otherwise stated. Prices are subject to change until full payment is received. Once paid in full, your rate is locked.

5. Late Payments

If the balance is not received by the due date, Brendava Tours reserves the right to cancel the booking and apply the cancellation fees outlined in our Cancellation Policy.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation by the Guest

If you need to cancel your booking, please notify us in writing (email is preferred). The following fees apply based on the date we receive your written notice:

Days Before Departure Cancellation Fee
60+ days Deposit only (30%)
45 – 59 days 50% of total tour price
30 – 44 days 75% of total tour price
Less than 30 days 100% of total tour price
No-show 100% of total tour price
Important: The deposit is non-refundable in all cases. Refunds (if any) will be processed within 14 business days to the original payment method.

Cancellation by Brendava Tours

In the rare event that we must cancel a tour due to insufficient numbers, force majeure, or other unforeseen circumstances, you will receive a full refund of all payments made, or the option to transfer to another date or tour of equal value.

Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance that covers trip cancellation, medical emergencies, and lost luggage. Brendava Tours is not responsible for any costs incurred due to cancellations outside our control.
