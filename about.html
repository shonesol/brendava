About Us

Brendava Tours & Travel is a trusted East African tour company committed to delivering unforgettable travel experiences across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. We specialize in wildlife safaris, gorilla trekking, bird watching, cultural encounters, mountain adventures, and tailor-made holiday packages designed to meet every traveler's dream.

Expert Safari Guides

Our knowledgeable guides provide exceptional wildlife interpretation and ensure every safari becomes a memorable adventure.

Customized Tours

Every traveler is unique. We create personalized itineraries tailored to your interests, schedule, and budget.

Safe & Reliable

We prioritize safety, comfort, and professional service throughout your journey from arrival to departure.

Our Mission

To provide authentic, sustainable, and life-changing travel experiences while supporting wildlife conservation and empowering local communities throughout East Africa.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading safari company, recognized globally for excellence, professionalism, and unforgettable adventures.

A Local Team You Can Trust

Based in Kampala, we work directly with experienced local guides, drivers and community partners across Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania. From your first enquiry to the final day of your safari, you deal with the same dedicated team — clear communication, transparent pricing and personal attention throughout.
